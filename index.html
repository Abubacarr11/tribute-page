Theo James

A tribute to an actor I admire

Biography

Theo James is an English actor, born in Oxford, England, in December 1984. He studied drama and began his career on stage and television before becoming known around the world for his film and TV roles.

I really admire someone who keeps a low profile and has a naturally mysterious personality, while also being multi-talented, versatile, and well-rounded. Someone who doesn’t feel the need to constantly show off or reveal everything about themselves, but whose different talents, interests, and qualities become more interesting the more you get to know them.

Career Highlights

  • 2012: Appeared in Downton Abbey as Kemal Pamuk
  • 2014: Starred as Four in Divergent
  • 2022: Played Cameron Sullivan in The White Lotus (Season 2)
  • 2024: Starred in the Netflix series The Gentlemen

In His Words

I'm a pessimist by nature, so I don't believe something until someone has kind of punched me over the head with it. - Theo James, Divergent
